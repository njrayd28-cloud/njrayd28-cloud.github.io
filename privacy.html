Privacy Policy

Noah Content Pipeline is a personal-use tool. It is used only by its developer, only with the developer's own accounts (YouTube, Instagram and TikTok).

What it accesses

With the developer's permission it can upload videos to the developer's own YouTube channel, post to the developer's own Instagram account, and send videos to the developer's own TikTok inbox as drafts. It reads the account name only to confirm which account is connected.

What it stores

Login tokens are stored on the developer's own computers and are never shared. The tool does not collect, store, sell or share personal data about any other person.
